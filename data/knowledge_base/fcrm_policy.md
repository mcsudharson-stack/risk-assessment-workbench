# Synthetic FCRM Risk Assessment Policy

This document is synthetic and is used only for the Risk Assessment Workbench hackathon demonstration.

## Money Laundering Risk

New products and material product changes must be assessed for potential money laundering exposure.

Factors include:
- movement of funds
- cross-border payments
- transaction volume
- transaction velocity
- customer type
- payment methods

Cross-border payment capabilities require enhanced review because funds may move between jurisdictions with different financial crime risk profiles.

## Sanctions Risk

Products supporting international transactions must be assessed for sanctions exposure.

The assessment should consider:
- countries involved
- counterparties
- payment destinations
- sanctions-screening controls
- transaction monitoring controls

Missing information about destination countries must be identified before final assessment.

## Customer Risk

Customer segments must be considered when determining inherent financial crime risk.

Higher-risk customer characteristics may require additional due diligence and monitoring.

The analyst must document the customer population affected by the proposed change.

## Geographic Risk

Changes involving new countries or international payment capabilities require geographic risk assessment.

The analyst should identify:
- countries where the product will operate
- countries where payments may be sent
- applicable geographic restrictions
- relevant sanctions exposure

## Third-Party Risk

When a third-party vendor is involved, the assessment must consider:
- vendor responsibilities
- access to customer or transaction data
- compliance responsibilities
- screening responsibilities
- monitoring responsibilities

Vendor involvement does not remove the bank's responsibility for financial crime risk management.

## Fraud Risk

New payment capabilities may introduce fraud risks including:
- unauthorized transactions
- account takeover
- transaction manipulation
- fraudulent beneficiaries

Relevant fraud prevention and monitoring controls should be documented.

## Human Decision Requirement

AI-generated analysis is advisory only.

AI must not approve or reject a change request.

The FCRM Analyst is responsible for reviewing and finalizing the assessment.

The Risk Committee retains responsibility for the final decision.

If a human reviewer disagrees with an AI-generated risk conclusion, the reviewer must document the reason for the override.
